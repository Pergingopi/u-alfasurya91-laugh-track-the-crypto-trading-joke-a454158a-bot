One line about crypto trading life: bought the dip, the dip dipped, and
now the portfolio is a museum of red candles while the group chat charges
admission to look at it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
The entry is validated first (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, single line, no
invisible or control characters), then published atomically with a
read-back check.

## Why this line won

The judge rewards a joke with a clear setup and a turn, a real laugh, and
a detail that feels true to trading life. "Bought the dip" is the setup
every trader has said out loud; "The dip dipped" turns it in four words;
the payoff — a museum of red candles, and a group chat charging admission
— is earned, visual, and repeatable to a friend at 3 a.m. in front of a
chart. Every word works, and the punchline lands at the end.

### Alternatives that lost

1. "My trading strategy is simple: buy high, sell low, and blame the chart. It has never once let me down." — the buy-high-sell-low line is the oldest joke in crypto; there is no surprise turn, only a familiar complaint restated.
2. "I told my mum crypto was the future. She asked why the future keeps crashing every time Elon tweets." — the Elon punchline is tired and predictable, and the joke spends its best words on the setup instead of the payoff.
3. "Diamond hands, paper profits, and a portfolio that only goes up in my dreams — at 3am, checking the chart like it owes me money." — it rambles across three images with no single turn, and the punchline arrives halfway through instead of at the end.
